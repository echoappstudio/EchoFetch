## EchoFetch 0.4.0

**Download just part of a video.** After a link is looked up, choose **Choose Part…** to watch the video in EchoFetch. Drag the start and end handles under the player, or type the times, then choose **Use This Part**. EchoFetch downloads only that stretch, so you don't get the whole file. This works for Download Video and Download Audio. If a site doesn't offer a preview EchoFetch can play, the site's own player appears instead, with **Set to Current Time** buttons.

**Smaller videos.** The new **Video quality** choice (on the Download tab and in Settings) has three options:

- **Original:** what EchoFetch has always saved.
- **Smaller (Apple HEVC):** compressed by your Mac's video encoder. Fast, good quality.
- **Smallest (x265):** the smallest files at good quality, but slower. A long 4K video can take a while.

The compressed choices download the best quality, compress it to HEVC (which QuickTime plays) and keep only the compressed copy. EchoFetch shows the compressing progress, and Cancel works there too. History shows the part and the compression for each download.
